# ENGLISH

## Before you start (2 minutes)

You will test two teaching simulators. They model a market the way physics
models a gas: price behaves like pressure, money behaves like gas, and a
piston settles at the market-clearing price.

- **What you need:** a web browser and the two simulator files (shared with
  you directly — please do not re-post or redistribute them).
- **What this is NOT:** not investment advice, not a stock forecast, not for
  real-money decisions. Everything you see is model-market behaviour, not a
  prediction about any real stock.
- **How to answer:** write down what you see and what you think, in your own
  words. There are no right or wrong answers — confusing parts are useful
  findings, not mistakes.

## Part A — Farmers-market simulator (about 12 minutes)

1. Open the farmers-market simulator file in your browser. Look around for
   one minute. Find the Demand slider, the Supply slider, and the piston in
   the middle. Write down the starting price.
2. **Demand shock:** press the "festival" scenario button (a sudden crowd of
   shoppers), or move the Demand slider up. Watch the piston. Does the price
   rise? Wait until the screen says "Piston at rest — market clears", then
   write down the new price.
3. **Supply shock:** press the "drought" scenario button (farmers bring less),
   or move the Supply slider down. Watch the piston again. Does the price
   move the way you expected? Wait until the piston rests, then write down
   the price.
4. **Your own shock:** pick any scenario button (sunny, rainy, glut) or move
   any slider. What happened? Did the piston settle, or keep wandering?
   Write down one sentence about it.
5. (Optional) Uncheck the "anchor" box, repeat one shock, and note what
   changes. Check the box again when you are done.

## Part B — Forecast simulator (about 10 minutes)

1. Open the forecast simulator file. Find the 80% cone (the band of likely
   outcomes), the white median path inside it, and the fair-value anchor
   line.
2. Look at the past week. Did the real price stay inside the 80% cone the
   whole week? If it stepped outside, write down when.
3. Where is the cone wide, and where is it narrow? A wide cone means the
   model is unsure; a narrow cone means it is more sure. Does that match
   what you would expect?
4. Write down one thing the cone got right and one thing it got wrong, if
   any.

## Part C — Five questions (about 5 minutes)

1. Could you run the simulators without help? If not, where did you get stuck?
2. What was confusing — a word, a button, a graph, an idea?
3. Did the piston price settle where you expected after a demand or supply shock?
4. What is the one thing you would change?
5. Would you use this to explain how a market works to a friend? Why or why not?

Send your answers to matthewy@mail.fresnostate.edu, or bring them to the
test session. Optional: stay 15 more minutes for a group discussion.

## Rules of the test

- Participation is voluntary and has no effect on any grade or club standing.
- Your feedback is for research only. It will be quoted only with your
  permission, and your name appears only if you agree.
- The simulators are shared with testers for testing, not for redistribution.

# 繁體中文

## 開始之前（2 分鐘）

你要測試兩個教學模擬器。它們把市場想成氣體：價格像氣壓，錢像氣體，活塞會停在市場成交的價錢。

- **你需要什麼：** 一個瀏覽器，和兩個模擬器檔案（直接分享給你，請不要轉貼或轉傳）。
- **這不是什麼：** 不是投資建議，不是股票預測，不是用來做真的買賣決定。你看到的一切都是模型裡市場的樣子，不是對任何真實股票的預測。
- **怎麼回答：** 用你自己的話，寫下你看到的和你想的。沒有對錯答案——看不懂的地方就是有用的發現，不是你的錯。

## 第一部分——農夫市場模擬器（大約 12 分鐘）

1. 在瀏覽器裡打開農夫市場模擬器。先看一分鐘，找到需求（Demand）的拉桿、供給（Supply）的拉桿，和中間的活塞。寫下開始時的價錢。
2. **需求衝擊：** 按「festival」（節日，突然來了很多買東西的人）這個情境按鈕，或是把需求拉桿往上拉。看著活塞，價錢上升了嗎？等到畫面寫著「Piston at rest — market clears」（活塞停住了，市場成交了），寫下新的價錢。
3. **供給衝擊：** 按「drought」（乾旱，農夫帶來的東西變少了）這個情境按鈕，或是把供給拉桿往下拉。再看一次活塞，價錢的變化跟你想的一樣嗎？等活塞停住，寫下價錢。
4. **你自己的衝擊：** 隨便選一個情境按鈕（sunny 晴天、rainy 下雨、glut 大豐收），或是動任何一個拉桿。發生了什麼事？活塞停住了，還是游來游去？用一句話寫下來。
5. （可以不做）把「anchor」（錨）的勾勾拿掉，再做一次衝擊，看看有什麼不一樣。做完再把勾勾打回來。

## 第二部分——預測模擬器（大約 10 分鐘）

1. 打開預測模擬器。找到 80% 圓錐（可能結果的範圍）、裡面白色的中間路線，和合理價值（fair value）的錨線。
2. 看過去一週，真實的價錢整週都留在 80% 圓錐裡面嗎？如果有跑出去，寫下是什麼時候。
3. 圓錐哪裡寬、哪裡窄？寬的地方表示模型不太確定，窄的地方表示模型比較確定。這跟你想的一樣嗎？
4. 寫下一件圓錐猜對的事，和一件猜錯的事（如果有的話）。

## 第三部分——五個問題（大約 5 分鐘）

1. 你可以自己跑這兩個模擬器，不用別人幫忙嗎？如果不行，你卡在哪裡？
2. 有什麼地方讓你看不懂——一個詞、一個按鈕、一張圖、一個想法？
3. 需求或供給受到衝擊之後，活塞的價錢停在你預期的地方嗎？
4. 如果只能改一件事，你會改什麼？
5. 你會用這個模擬器向朋友解釋市場是怎麼運作的嗎？為什麼會／為什麼不會？

請把答案寄到 matthewy@mail.fresnostate.edu，或是帶到測試時間來。也可以多留 15 分鐘，一起討論。

## 測試規則

- 參加完全自願，不影響任何成績或社團地位。
- 你的回饋只用來做研究。引用你的話之前會先問你，寫你的名字之前也會先問你。
- 模擬器只分享給測試的人測試，不可以轉傳給別人。
