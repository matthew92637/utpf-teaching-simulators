How to use (short version)

  1. Press Normal day first. Note Y* and r*.
  2. Press Recession: confidence falls, IS shifts left, Y and r fall.
  3. Press Government stimulus: IS shifts right, Y rises — and r rises too.
  4. Press Central bank prints money: LM shifts right, r falls, Y rises.
  5. Press Liquidity trap: r is stuck at 0. Then press print +$50 — almost nothing happens. Then press spend +$20 — output jumps. That is the Keynesian lesson in one minute.
  6. Move the sliders by hand and watch the dot and the piston follow.
Honest limits — read before believing anything:
  • This is a teaching model, not a forecast. Short run only; the price level is fixed, the economy is closed, and the lines are straight on purpose.
  • The gas numbers are a translation of the IS-LM numbers, not a measurement of a real market. Z here is computed from the mapping (Z = ΠV/(QT)); it is a way to see the model, not a law of nature.
  • Nothing here is investment advice. Do not buy or sell anything from these numbers.
