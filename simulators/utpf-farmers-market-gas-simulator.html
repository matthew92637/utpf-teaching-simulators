🧑‍🌾 UTPF Farmers Market — the Gas-Model Simulator

One market, two gas chambers — Demand (shoppers carrying coins) on the left, Supply (farmers with crates of goods) on the right — separated by the market counter, a free piston. The counter slides until the two pressures balance. Where the counter rests is the price. No supply or demand curves are drawn and no elasticities are fitted: the market obeys an equation of state, Π·V = Z·Q·T, and price formation, shortage, and glut all fall out of the gas.

How to read the market counter

Each dot is a person at the market, bouncing like a gas molecule: blue shoppers carrying coins on the left, orange farmers with crates on the right. Dots run faster when their chamber is hot (T = how eager and scattered choices are). Pressure Π is measured from the force of dots hitting the walls and the counter — nothing is assumed — and Z = ΠV / (QT) scores each chamber against an ideal gas. If shopper pressure beats farmer pressure, the counter slides right: shoppers gain room, the farmers are squeezed, and the price (read on the ruler above) rises. Squeezing a chamber at fixed T raises its pressure — Boyle's law — until the two sides balance again. That resting point is market clearing, drawn as physics. Trades happen at the counter: watch a crate fly left and a coin fly right, one for one. The gold spring behind the counter is custom — the memory of the customary price — pulling the market back toward it.

Five principles this market demonstrates

1 · Price is a balance, not a decree. The counter rests where the two pressures are equal — that rest point is the market-clearing price.
2 · Shortage squeezes. Shrink the farmers' room (drought, sold out) and their pressure climbs by Boyle's law; the price must rise until balance returns.
3 · A glut sags. Flood the supply chamber and each farmer presses less; the counter drifts the other way and prices soften.
4 · Goods and money flow against each other. Nothing is created at the counter: crates go one way, coins come back, and the totals always match.
5 · Z is the market's health number. Near 1 on a calm day; above 1 when the crowd packs in (festival); below 1 when the market freezes and herds.

UTPF Farmers Market — gas-model edition · kinetic core: pressure is measured from wall and counter collisions over a rolling ~4-second window; Z is computed from the measured state (Π, V, Q, T), never assumed, and no supply/demand curves or elasticities are used anywhere in the instrument. Trades at the counter conserve goods and currency exactly — audit the two flows in the panel. A moving counter does work on the gas, so a fast shove briefly heats the squeezed chamber before the market settles. A market is not literally a gas: people read the weather and the news; the thermostat and the customary-price spring stand for that.
